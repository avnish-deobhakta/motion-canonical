# DR6 amendment 3: decision-model grades for the audited descriptions (17 October 2026, after the amendment 2 review)

**Status.** Post hoc. Written after the amendment 2 review results (commit a24837c43a) were seen. No new model calls: it reads Jev's and the self-pass grades already frozen in stage 2 (freeze commit a1890d51) for the 60 audited descriptions. Reported as exploratory; changes no earlier result.

**Question.** For the misses the specialist confirmed as referable, would converting the model's own description with Jev (or the self-pass) have produced a referral?

**Data.** The 60 cases of amendment 2, joined by model and image to the stage-2 Jev grade and self-pass grade of the same free-text description, and to the specialist's codes. A grade of 2 to 4, "ungradable" or no readable answer counts as a referral, as in the primary analysis. For Qwen3.8-27B, Jev read the full description including reasoning (amendment 1); the self-pass grade uses the amended parser.

**Groups.**
* Confirmed referable: specialist grade 2 to 4 (n = 34), split into *described*: at least one lesion other than microaneurysms coded D (n = 24), and *not described*: none coded D (n = 10).
* Reference not supported: specialist grade 0 or 1 (n = 19).
* Ungradable on review (n = 7).

**Outcomes (descriptive; Wilson 95% CIs; no tests).** In each group, the share of cases Jev would have referred, and the same for the self-pass. Primary interest: Jev referral in the *described* group.

**Limitations.** As for amendment 2 (single grader, post hoc, 60 cases), and this analysis was specified after the review results were known.
